Rules and facts

1. How a stage works

  • Three stages, one for each lesson: Lesson 3 What are cells?, Lesson 5 Specialised cells and Lesson 6 Cell transport. Start zooming plays them in order, or tap one stage.
  • Each stage opens with a short briefing. Every part has a task: predict, drag, run a model or explore a diagram. Next unlocks when you have done it.
  • Then a focus check. Pass it to unlock the quiz. Get it wrong and you reread a part and get a different check question.
  • Mission: 10 questions that adapt to you. Question 10 is a boss question in the style of the eAssessment, worth double points.
  • After your first Mission, Time attack (two minutes, as many as you can) and Fix my mistakes (questions you got wrong before, half points) unlock.

2. The zoom

  • Five levels: 1 Body, 2 Organ, 3 Tissue, 4 Cell, 5 Molecules. The view zooms in or out as you go, with a real scale bar at every level.
  • Every Mission starts at level 3, the tissue.
  • Two correct answers in a row zoom you in one level. One wrong answer zooms you out one level.
  • Questions you got wrong before can come back later, so learn from the solution.

3. The clock

  • Every question has a typical time.
  • Relaxed: the clock keeps running past it and you can still score. Exam pace: when the time runs out the question scores zero.
  • Pause stops the clock. Opening Stickers, Scores, Progress or Rules mid-quiz pauses it too.

4. Points and stars

  • Correct: 20 points for each level of the question (level 3 = 60), plus bonuses. A hint halves the points for that question.
  • Pace bonus +10 within 60% of the typical time, +5 within it. Streak bonus +5, +10, up to +20.
  • Boss questions score double. Time attack scores 10 per level. Fix my mistakes scores half.
  • Every 100 points gives one star to spend on stickers.

5. Answering

  • Drag a word or label into a box, or tap it and then tap the box. Every box must be right.
  • Numbers are accepted within 2%. Type just the number; negative numbers need the minus sign.
  • Some questions give new numbers every time, so copying someone else's answer will not work.
  • Model questions: set the controls, then press Check.
  • Tap Read aloud to hear a question or lesson part.

6. Playing fair

  • Quizzes run in full screen. Lessons and checks are not locked.
  • During a quiz question, leaving the game means switching tab, app, window or desktop, or leaving full screen (Esc).
  • Leaving voids the question (no answer is shown), zooms you out a level and costs up to 50 points.
  • The third time in one quiz ends it, and quizzes are locked for 10 minutes.
  • Your teacher sees your answers to every quiz question, your progress on each objective, every exit, and very fast correct answers on hard questions. Copying questions is blocked.
  • Your teacher may ask for a class code before a quiz.
  • Notifications can pull you out of the game too, so turn them off first.

7. Stickers, scores, progress

  • Spend stars in Stickers and wear one as your badge.
  • Scores is shared by the class and shows points, accuracy and exits. Only the teacher can reset it.
  • Progress shows your best result, your three objectives per stage and your achievements.

8. Saving

  • Everything is saved in this browser under your name. Use the same name to carry on.
  • Leaving mid-round keeps your place; tap Play or Resume to return.
  • Clearing the browser data or using another device starts afresh.
